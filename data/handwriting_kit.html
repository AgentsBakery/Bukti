Bukti handwriting kit

What this is. Each section below is one loan applicant. Copy each page into a real paper notebook (school exercise book is perfect), then photograph it. The numbers must match exactly; the descriptions can be reworded naturally in the same language. Dates can be written short (1/9, 2/9...).
Photos. One photo per page, daylight or a bright room, whole page in frame, a slight tilt is fine (it makes the demo more real). Save as data/packs/<id>/photos/page1.jpg and page2.jpg. Hero packs (sari, murugan) also need: 1 photo of a small shop shelf or kitchen shelf (shop.jpg) and 1-2 handwritten supplier receipts for any two expense lines (receipt1.jpg).
Notebook columns. Date | Description | In (money received) | Out (money spent).
